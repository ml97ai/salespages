## APPENDIX: COPY PRINCIPLES APPLIED

Every piece of copy in this blueprint follows Alen Sultanic's frameworks:

1. **TSL 3-Stage Model:** Problem Agreement (20%) → Solution Agreement (40%) → Product Agreement (40%)
2. **AIDVVA:** Attention → Interest → Desire → Verification → Validation → Action
3. **Desire + Justification = Action:** Desire is built through problem/solution sections. Justification through ROI math, guarantee, and value stack.
4. **POV Change:** The entire sales page changes their point of view from "I need a better strategy" to "I need to know my numbers"
5. **Absolution of Responsibility:** "It's not your strategy. It's not your discipline." → Software does the hard work.
6. **Risk Reversal Infused:** Throughout the copy, not just stacked at the end. "One payout pays for it 4x over" appears before the guarantee section.
7. **Binding Statements:** "If any of this sounds like you..." — they can't say no.
8. **Identity Close:** "If you're someone who's already put in the work to learn a strategy..." — saying no means rejecting themselves.
9. **Consequence Close:** "Every day you trade without knowing your numbers is another day you're leaving money on the table."
10. **Format Match:** VSL for high-intent viewers (who watch). Long-form text below for skeptics (who read). Both paths lead to the same CTA.
